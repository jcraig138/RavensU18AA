Glossary

Every term, abbreviation and symbol on this dashboard. Hover a column header, row label or stat box anywhere on the page for the same definition. Type to filter.

Results & records

GP
Games played.
W · L · T
Wins, losses, ties.
W-L-T (record)
Wins-losses-ties written as one record, e.g. 13-7-5.
Result
W, L or T and the final score, Ravens goals first (W 4-3 means the Ravens won 4 to 3).
GF · Goals for
Goals the Ravens scored.
GA · Goals against
Goals the Ravens allowed.
GF/GP · GA/GP
Goals for and against per game played. The average number scored and allowed each game.
Pts%
Points percentage. Points earned out of the most possible (2 for a win, 1 for a tie), counting every game in the split.

Standings

#
Place in the division, in the order the OWHA lists it (tie-breaks are the OWHA's). In the All teams view it is the place across the whole league: ordered by points, then wins, then Win%, then goal differential.
Team
Team name as the OWHA lists it. The Ravens' row is highlighted.
PTS
Standings points. 2 for a win, 1 for a tie, 0 for a loss.
DIFF
Goal differential. Goals for minus goals against. Positive means a team has scored more than it has allowed.
PIM
Penalty minutes. Total minutes of penalties a team has been assessed in league games. Lower means a more disciplined team (and fewer penalties to kill).
Win%
Win percentage, exactly as the OWHA site shows it: (wins + half of ties) ÷ games played, which is the same as points ÷ points possible. A 3-0-1 record is 0.875.
Ravens vs
The Ravens' record (W-L-T) against that team across every game this season, league or not. Blank means we haven't played them.
Division
OWHA U18 AA divisions: Central North, East, South, Southwest and West. The buttons above the table switch between them, and All teams stacks every team in the league into one list with a Division column. The Ravens' division opens first.
League game
An OWHA regular-season game. Only these count in the standings. Exhibitions and tournament games count in the Ravens' own record but not in the standings.

Shots & expected goals

Shot attempts
Every shot the Ravens took: on goal, missed the net, or blocked.
Shots on goal
Shots that reached the goalie or went in. Misses and blocked shots don't count.
SC · Scoring chances
Shots from InStat's scoring-chance area, the slot and between the inner hash marks, where most goals come from.
Sh% · Shooting %
Shooting percentage. Goals ÷ shots on goal. A team norm is roughly 8–11%; far above or below usually evens out over time.
xG
Expected goals. InStat's estimate of how many goals the shots should produce, based on where they came from, the type of shot and the situation. Measures chance quality, not luck.
xGF · xG for
Expected goals for: the xG of the Ravens' shots.
xGA · xG against
Expected goals against: the xG of the opponent's shots.
xGF% · xG share
Share of expected goals. xGF ÷ (xGF + xGA). Above 50% means the Ravens are creating the better chances.
Goals − xG
Finishing and goaltending against expectation. For: goals scored minus xG for (below zero = scoring fewer than our chances were worth). Against: goals allowed minus xG against (below zero = our goalies are stopping more than expected).

Possession & faceoffs

CF% · Corsi
Shot-attempt share. Ravens shot attempts ÷ all shot attempts by both teams. Above 50% means the Ravens have the puck and are shooting more than the opponent.
FO% · Faceoff %
Faceoff win percentage. Share of faceoffs the Ravens win.

Special teams

PP · Power play
Ravens have more skaters because the opponent took a penalty. In a game it's shown as goals / chances, e.g. 1/3 = one power-play goal on three chances.
PP%
Power-play percentage. Power-play goals ÷ power-play chances, counted over the whole period rather than averaging each game.
PK · Penalty kill
Ravens are shorthanded because we took a penalty. In a game it's shown as kills / times shorthanded, e.g. 3/4 = killed three of four penalties.
PK%
Penalty-kill percentage. Penalties killed without allowing a goal ÷ times shorthanded. 85% or better is a solid kill.
SH · Times shorthanded
How many penalties the Ravens had to kill.

Splits & trends

Split
A slice of the season the same stats are totalled over, so you can compare one part of the season with another.
Season
Every game played so far.
August · September · October
Games played in that month.
First 10 · Last 10 · Last 5
The first ten games of the season, and the ten or five most recent.
vs AA · vs AAA
Games against AA teams (our level) and against AAA teams (the level above).
Home · Away (venue)
Where the game was played: our rink or theirs.
Rolling average
An average over a game and the four tracked games before it. It smooths out single good or bad games so the direction is easier to see.
Trending
The last 5 tracked games against the 5 before them. The big number is the last-5 value; the small line is the 5-game rolling value across the season, with the dashed line at the season figure.
Improving · Slipping · Steady
Direction of a trend. Improving or Slipping when the last 5 differ from the 5 before by at least 2 points (5 points for power play and penalty kill); otherwise Steady.
pts (percentage points)
The difference between two percentages. Going from 50% to 53% is +3 pts.
Season avg
Season average of that stat per game (InStat stats over tracked games; power play and penalty kill over all chances).

Reading the Games tab

G · G15
Game number in season order. G15 is the 15th game.
Date
Month-day the game was played (10-19 = 19 October).
Opponent
Who the Ravens played. (AAA) or (16U AAA) marks a team from a higher level.
▲ ▼
Clearly better or worse than the season average for that stat. Green is good for the Ravens, red is bad (fewer goals against is good, so it gets ▲ in green).
Underlined value
The best of the games you picked for that stat.
Game chips
Buttons for each game. The coloured left edge is the result: green win, red loss, yellow tie. Pick up to six to compare.
Season strip
Where each game sits in the season. Grey dots are every tracked game, the short line is the season average, and coloured dots are the games you picked.
Tracked game
A game with InStat video stats. Every game except Stratford (26 Sep), which has the result only, so its xG, shots, faceoffs and special teams show as —.
—
No data for that game or stat.
